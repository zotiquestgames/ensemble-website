Core Rules

What Is Ensemble?

Ensemble is a fiction engine: a roleplaying game where fiction precedes mechanics. Characters, places and things are tags. You stay in the conversation, and you roll only when the outcome is uncertain and failure matters.

It follows four design principles:

Portable
A handful of six-sided dice and something to write with.
Rules-Light
One core mechanic covers almost everything. You learn it in minutes.
Tag-Based
Characters, places and things are described in words, not numbers.
Emergent
No pre-written plot. Every session generates its own story.

Modes of Play

Classic

One player is the Game Master, who manages the world and all the NPCs. The others each play a character.

GM-less

No Game Master. The players share narrative authority, with a rotating scene host or fully collective, and the Oracle drives the story.

For solo play, see Loner, the standalone game built on the same foundation. You do not need one to play the other.
