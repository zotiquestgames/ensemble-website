How To Play

Ensemble is a conversation. Someone frames a scene, the characters act, and the world answers. When the outcome is uncertain and failure matters, the dice decide.

The Basics

01
Build a Character with Tags
A Concept, two Skills and a Frailty, plus a Motive, a long-term Goal, some Gear, and a phrase about another character and about an NPC. No numbers.
02
Frame a Scene
Where are we, who is there, what is going on. The scene closes when its goal is resolved, blocked or abandoned.
03
Ask and Build the Pool
Turn the action into a yes-or-no question and say what the fiction suggests. Take the Action Die, add a Chance Die for each tag that helps and a Risk Die for each one that hinders, then cancel them one for one.
04
Roll and Interpret
Keep the highest die if Chance Dice remain, the lowest if Risk Dice remain. The result tells you what happens and where the story turns.

Six Outcomes

6
Yes, and… Success with an advantage.
5
Yes… Success.
4
Yes, but… Success at a cost. Add 1 to the Twist Counter.
3
No, but… Failure with a consolation.
2
No… Failure.
1
No, and… Failure with a disadvantage.

The table shares one Twist Counter. When it reaches 3, roll on the Twist Table and reset it: something changes in the story that nobody planned.

Try It

Roll a Dice Pool

Phrase a yes-or-no question, count the tags that help and the tags that hinder, then roll.

Conflicts

Zoom Out
Resolve the whole conflict with a single question.
Zoom In
Play it out in phases, one question at a time.
Harm & Luck
Optional. Both sides start at 6 Luck; the conflict ends when one side reaches 0.
