Ensemble

One table. One story nobody wrote in advance.

Ensemble is a rules-light roleplaying game for two or more players. You describe what your character does, the world reacts, and the dice come in only when the outcome is uncertain or dangerous. Every rule exists to move the story forward.

Play in Classic mode, with a Game Master who runs the world, or GM-less, with the group sharing narrative authority and the Oracle filling the gaps. All you need is a handful of six-sided dice in three colors and something to write with.

Tags

Characters, places and things are described in words, not numbers. A tag adds a die only when it bears on what is happening.

The Dice Pool

Ask a yes-or-no question. Roll the Action Die with a Chance Die for each tag that helps, a Risk Die for each one that hinders, and read one of six answers.

Playing Loner?
Ensemble shares the Oracle, the tags and the six outcomes with Loner 4e, the solo game. Your Loner characters and Adventure Packs carry over with a few adjustments, and the book explains them. Want to play alone? Loner is the game for that.
